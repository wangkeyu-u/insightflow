# Product

## Register

product

## Users

InsightFlow serves operations leaders, sales managers, finance staff, inventory coordinators, and administrators at a mid-sized B2B workplace-technology distributor. They use it from a desktop during recurring operational reviews to understand commercial performance, investigate exceptions, maintain master data, and export evidence for follow-up. A secondary audience is a technical interviewer evaluating the quality of the product and its engineering decisions.

## Product Purpose

InsightFlow combines orders, accounts, products, inventory, receivables, fulfillment, regional targets, reports, audit history, and assisted analysis in one operational workspace. Success means a user can move from a high-level signal to the relevant record, understand why the signal exists, take the next action, and leave an auditable trail without switching tools.

## Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, credible. The product should feel like a calm operations console: information-rich without being noisy, familiar without being generic, and explicit about data quality and system state.

## Anti-references

- Randomly generated demo dashboards whose metrics cannot be reconciled across pages.
- Template-like admin panels with flat hierarchy, inconsistent button placement, or decorative gradients.
- Consumer-style playful UI, oversized marketing typography, glass effects, and excessive animation.
- Mobile-first compromises that reduce desktop data density; mobile layout is intentionally out of scope.

## Design Principles

1. Make every metric traceable to an operational record or documented calculation.
2. Place the primary action consistently and keep destructive actions visually subordinate.
3. Prefer dense, familiar desktop workflows over decorative novelty.
4. Expose exceptions early: overdue receivables, target gaps, and low stock should be easy to locate and explain.
5. Preserve trust through clear states, permission boundaries, auditability, and honest AI fallbacks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard-operable controls. Do not rely on color alone for status, retain visible focus states, provide accessible names for icon buttons, and respect reduced-motion preferences. The supported surface is desktop at 1180px and above.
